What this is

vercel.school is Adam Pang's independent proof-of-work classroom for people evaluating how he builds and ships on Vercel. The public curriculum explains six practical lessons, and every lesson links to a real deployed project that visitors can inspect themselves.

The classroom and product directory are free to browse, with no paid plan or course checkout. Teams that want hands-on Vercel onboarding can contact Adam to describe their needs and request a quote; everyone else can use the lessons and linked products without signing up.

Curriculum

every lesson is a shipped artifact, not a slide →
Lesson 01

Ship in an afternoon

The first shipping lesson covers the complete loop from idea to repository to vercel deploy --prod. Practicing that loop matters more than polishing slides, because every later lesson improves a process that already puts working software online.

proof: a live product directory ↗
Lesson 02

Domains are distribution

The domains lesson explains apex and www records, permanent redirects, and DNS maintenance for a large portfolio. It treats each domain as a distribution decision and shows why unused registrations create recurring cost and operational confusion.

proof: a federation of live domains ↗
Lesson 03

AI is your build crew

The AI build-crew lesson uses tools such as Claude, v0, and Lovable as collaborators while keeping human review in the loop. The goal is faster execution with editorial judgment, working links, and production verification.

proof: built with the crew ↗
Lesson 04

Preview deploys are the classroom

The preview-deploy lesson gives each proposed change a shareable URL before production. Reviewers can test the real interface instead of interpreting a mockup, so feedback arrives against working behavior and demos become concrete homework.

proof: an operating dashboard, live ↗
Lesson 05

The edge is close to your users

The edge lesson starts from Adam's APAC working context at Network School in Malaysia. It asks builders to consider where users actually live and to choose delivery infrastructure that keeps the experience close to them.

proof: civic tools for a network school ↗
Lesson 06

Ship or it didn't happen

The final lesson treats a portfolio as proof-of-work rather than a résumé or slide deck. Public URLs let visitors inspect the result directly, and vercel.school serves as its own final exam by remaining deployed and usable.

proof: the teacher ↗
