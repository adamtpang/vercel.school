About vercel.school

vercel.school is an independent working classroom created and operated by Adam Pang. The site explains six practical lessons about shipping with Vercel, then links each lesson to deployed work so visitors can evaluate the evidence instead of relying on a résumé or sales deck.

What visitors can do here

Visitors can read the curriculum, open every proof link, and try the 11 products featured in the public directory. Browsing is free, there is no course checkout, and subscribing to updates is optional.

Who operates the site

Adam Pang maintains vercel.school from his APAC working context and links his public builder profile at adampang.com. Teams considering hands-on Vercel onboarding can use the contact page to describe what they need.

Relationship to Vercel

vercel.school is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Vercel Inc. The Vercel name describes the platform used and taught by this independent project.
