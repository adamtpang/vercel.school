## Pond notes

- r/SideProject: high signal for exactly this ask, low self-promo friction
  since the post leads with "give me feedback" not "buy my thing." Verify the
  sub's current self-promo/frequency rule before posting.
- Indie Hackers Show IH: slower replies but higher-intent readers, good
  second pond once the Reddit post has run its course, not both same day.
- Do not post to r/webdev or Hacker News with this exact copy: both read
  "40+ products" as a spam signal without a single deep technical hook to
  anchor it. Revisit with a narrower, single-product angle if targeting those.
